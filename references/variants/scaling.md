# Variant: scaling

How behaviour changes with scale (data, parameters, compute, context).

This variant changes how the protocols in [protocols.md](../protocols.md) apply to this task type. It never switches one off. Seeds below are starting vocabulary; the study's `rubric` and `test_protocol` assets are rebuilt from this field's evidence (see [design](../../docs/design.md) §2–3).

| Protocol | How it applies |
|---|---|
| Falsification (3) | A pre-stated functional form or break point, fitted on small scales and predicted at a held-out larger one; the rival form predicts a different held-out value. |
| Review | Extrapolation range honesty; compute envelope from `environment`; whether the fitted form is identifiable from the points available. |
| Deliverable | A plan with the held-out prediction, or verified execution at affordable scales. |

Watch for: fitting and evaluating on the same points; scales beyond the authorized budget.
