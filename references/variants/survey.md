# Variant: survey

A survey or landscape: what exists, how it relates, where the gaps are.

This variant changes how the protocols in [protocols.md](../protocols.md) apply to this task type. It never switches one off. Seeds below are starting vocabulary; the study's `rubric` and `test_protocol` assets are rebuilt from this field's evidence (see [design](../../docs/design.md) §2–3).

| Protocol | How it applies |
|---|---|
| Falsification (3) | The taxonomy must predict where **unseen** papers fall: hold out a recent set, classify it before reading, then check. The rival is a simpler taxonomy that classifies as well. |
| Reading (1) | Coverage is claimed only over what was retrieved and read; the retrieval plan states its sources and windows, and an empty search is not a gap. |
| Review | Coverage, taxonomy novelty, whether claimed gaps are gaps or retrieval misses. |
| Deliverable | A report. |

Watch for: a gap that is a search failure; counting papers as independent when they are one family.
